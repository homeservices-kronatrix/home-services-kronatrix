England planning overview

Do you need planning permission for a swimming pool?

Do not rely on a blanket yes/no answer. In England, some outdoor domestic pools can fall within permitted-development rules, but the property, location, associated buildings and local restrictions can change the position.

Direct answer: an outdoor domestic pool may sometimes be permitted development in England, but that does not mean every swimming-pool project is automatically permission-free. Listed buildings, designated land, conservation controls, local restrictions and the construction of an indoor pool building can materially affect what is required.

Outdoor pool

Check whether the work falls within the relevant permitted-development conditions for the property and location. Do not assume an online example proves your own project qualifies.

Indoor pool / pool building

A new building introduces a separate set of planning and building considerations. Size, position, height, use and the property's status all matter.

Special property/location status

Listed buildings, conservation areas and designated land can change the planning route and the advice required.

Building and technical requirements

Planning permission and Building Regulations are different systems. Structural, electrical, ventilation, drainage and other technical requirements should be checked with the relevant professionals.

Important
