PRIVACY & ANALYTICS

How Home Services uses analytics.

KRONATRIX Home Services uses Google Analytics to understand how the website is used and to improve its pages, navigation and calls to action. Google Analytics is loaded on this site only after a visitor chooses Allow analytics.

What we measure

Analytics can record page views and selected website interactions such as clicks on the Home Services email link, visibility-review calls to action, the pool-business visibility page and the live proof-site link. These events are used to understand website performance and improve the visitor journey.

What we do not send through these events

The website does not send your email address, the contents of an email message, or other user-entered contact information to Google Analytics through the custom events installed on this site.

Advertising settings

The Home Services implementation keeps advertising storage, advertising user-data consent and advertising personalisation disabled. Google Signals and ad-personalisation signals are also disabled in the website configuration.

Your choice

Your analytics choice is remembered locally in your browser so the website can respect it on later visits. You can change the choice at any time by using the Analytics settings control at the bottom of the website.

Email contact

Email links open your own email application. If you choose to send an email, it is sent to homeservices.kronatrix@gmail.com.
