Indoor swimming pool cost: what makes it different?

An indoor pool budget includes both the pool and the environment around it. The pool hall, ventilation, humidity control, thermal performance, heating and plant-room design can be major parts of the project, so an outdoor-pool price is not a reliable indoor-pool benchmark.

Explore pool researchFor pool businesses
Direct answer: Indoor swimming pool cost is shaped by the pool itself plus the building and environmental-control systems needed to manage warm water, humid air and year-round use. The project may need coordinated architectural, structural, mechanical, electrical and pool-specialist design.

Think of it as two systems that must work together

The pool

Structure, waterproofing or shell, filtration, treatment, heating, covers, controls and finishes.

The pool hall

Envelope, ventilation, dehumidification, thermal performance, condensation control, access and plant space.

Why ventilation and thermal performance matter

Planning Portal specifically flags ventilation and thermal performance for indoor pool buildings. Current Approved Documents F and L provide the wider England building-regulation framework for ventilation and energy performance where applicable.
