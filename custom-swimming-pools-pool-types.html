Pool-type decision

Concrete, liner or one-piece pool: compare the system before the finish.

Pool types should be compared by the decisions they create: design freedom, installation approach, finish options, site constraints, future maintenance and repairability.

Direct answer: there is no universally best construction system. A reinforced concrete pool can suit highly bespoke forms; liner and one-piece systems use different construction and finishing approaches. The right choice depends on the site, design brief, programme, finish expectations and long-term ownership plan.

Do not confuse construction system with pool purpose.

“Infinity”, “lap”, “plunge”, “indoor” and “outdoor” describe use, setting or design characteristics. They are not always separate structural systems.

Infinity / overflow

A hydraulic and architectural design choice that affects edge detailing, balance tanks and site design.

Lap / exercise

Purpose drives length, lane geometry, depth and optional counter-current systems.

Plunge / compact

Smaller-footprint projects still need proper structure, circulation, treatment and safe access.
