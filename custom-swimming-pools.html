What is a custom swimming pool?
A custom or bespoke swimming pool is planned around a particular property, intended use and specification rather than selected only as a fixed off-the-shelf package. The real decision includes the site, construction system, design, plant, heating, permissions, maintenance and contractor responsibilities.

The six decisions to resolve first

Purpose

Exercise, family use, relaxation, training, entertaining or a mixed requirement changes the brief.

Site

Space, access, ground conditions, relationship to the house and plant location all affect feasibility.

Pool type

The construction system affects design flexibility, installation method, finish choices and maintenance.

Budget structure

Separate shell/build cost from plant, finishes, heating, cover, surrounding works, professional input and ongoing operation.

Permissions and responsibilities

Confirm planning, building and professional requirements for the actual property instead of relying on a blanket rule.

Contractor selection

Compare scope, exclusions, evidence, responsibilities, commissioning, warranties and aftercare—not only headline price.
