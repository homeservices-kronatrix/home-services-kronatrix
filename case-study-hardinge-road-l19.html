Reusable lessons

What carries forward into KRONATRIX Home Services.

Decision-first architecture

The property site was organised around the questions and evidence a buyer needed before deciding whether to view. Home Services applies the same principle to complex service purchases.

Verified-answer discipline

The project's buyer-help search was designed to answer only from confirmed facts and to refuse unsupported answers. The same evidence discipline applies to pool costs, planning and technical guidance.

Measurement beyond traffic

The project tracked actions such as calls, agent contact, viewing clicks, gallery use, maps, video and answered/unanswered questions. Home Services will likewise define meaningful actions before installing analytics.
