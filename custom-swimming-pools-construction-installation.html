INSTALLATION & CONSTRUCTION

How is a swimming pool installed?

Pool installation is a coordinated construction project rather than a single installation event. The sequence varies by pool system and site, but most projects move through survey and brief, design and checks, groundworks, structure or shell installation, services, finishes, commissioning and handover.

Explore pool researchFor pool businesses
Direct answer: A typical custom-pool project starts with the brief and site, then technical design and permission checks, excavation and structural work, pool and service installation, finishes and surrounding works, followed by testing, commissioning and handover. The exact order and duration depend on the system, site and project team.

A practical project sequence

Brief, survey and feasibility

Define intended use, size, site constraints, access, plant position and project responsibilities.

Design and checks

Coordinate pool design, structure, equipment, utilities, permissions and surrounding works.

Groundworks and pool structure

Excavation, base preparation, drainage and the selected structural or shell system.

Services and finishes

Filtration, treatment, heating, electrics, lighting, covers, finishes and pool surrounds.

Commissioning and handover

Testing, balancing, controls, operating instructions, warranty information and maintenance plan.

What commonly changes the timeline

Ground conditions, site access, weather, design changes, planning/building-control requirements, specialist availability, equipment lead times and dependencies with landscaping or building work can all affect sequencing.
