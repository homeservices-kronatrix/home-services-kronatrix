Build process

How is a custom swimming pool built and installed?

The exact sequence depends on the pool system and site, but a responsible project normally moves through definition, surveys/design, permissions, site preparation, structure, services, finishing, commissioning and handover.

Direct answer: construction starts well before excavation. The design, site, responsibilities, permissions and plant strategy should be understood first. Installation then coordinates groundworks, structure/shell, hydraulics, filtration, heating, electrical work, finishes and final commissioning.

Define the brief

Purpose, size, depth, indoor/outdoor setting, features, finish expectations and budget structure.

Assess the site

Access, ground conditions, levels, drainage, relationship to buildings and plant location.

Design and responsibilities

Confirm design ownership, structural input, equipment specification and who coordinates other trades.

Permissions

Check the actual property and project before assuming permitted development or other exemptions apply.

Excavation and structure

Sequence depends on whether the pool is site-built or uses a manufactured system.

Plant and services

Install circulation, filtration, heating, controls, pipework and associated electrical systems to the approved design.

Finish and surrounds

Complete internal finish, edge details, cover, drainage and surrounding construction/landscaping.

Commission and hand over

Test systems, balance water, demonstrate operation, document maintenance and confirm warranties/aftercare.

Why exact build times are risky to promise

Programme depends on project complexity, pool system, site access, ground conditions, design changes, permissions, other trades, weather and equipment lead times. A contractor should explain the assumptions behind its programme.

Ask for milestones

A useful programme separates design/approvals, enabling work, main construction, plant installation, finishes, commissioning and handover.

Ask who coordinates

Responsibility gaps between pool contractor, builder, electrician, landscaper and other professionals can create delay and cost.
