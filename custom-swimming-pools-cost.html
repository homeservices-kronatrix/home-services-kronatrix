Cost decision

How much does a custom swimming pool cost in the UK?

There is no responsible single price for a custom pool without defining the project. A useful estimate starts by separating the pool itself from site work, plant, heating, covers, finishes, surrounding construction, professional input and future running costs.

Evidence-led frameworkNo fabricated average
Direct answer: custom swimming pool cost can vary materially because “a swimming pool” is not one specification. Indoor/outdoor setting, size, access, ground conditions, construction type, finish, plant, heating and associated building work can change the project substantially. Compare quotes by scope, not headline total alone.

Build the budget in layers

1. Site and structure

  • surveys and design input
  • access and excavation
  • ground conditions
  • structural shell / pool system
  • waterproofing and finishes

2. Pool systems

  • circulation and filtration
  • heating
  • cover and cover mechanism
  • lighting / controls
  • plant room and pipework

3. Associated works

  • surrounds and drainage
  • landscaping
  • indoor building/enclosure where applicable
  • ventilation/dehumidification where applicable
  • electrical and specialist trades

4. Ownership

  • energy
  • water treatment
  • cleaning and servicing
  • seasonal winterisation where relevant
  • repairs and replacement components

Questions a useful quotation should answer

What is included?

Confirm shell, plant, finishes, cover, heating, commissioning and handover items individually.

What is excluded?

Clarify access work, landscaping, drainage, professional fees, planning work, electrical work and building/enclosure work.

What changes the price?

Understand provisional sums, site assumptions, variations and who bears the risk when conditions differ.
