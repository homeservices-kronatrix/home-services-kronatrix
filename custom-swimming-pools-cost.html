POOL COST

How much does a swimming pool cost in the UK?

There is no single defensible UK price for a custom residential swimming pool because the specification and site can change the project substantially. This guide focuses on the variables you should price explicitly rather than publishing a misleading universal average.

Explore pool researchFor pool businesses
Direct answer: The cost of a custom pool is driven by the pool system and dimensions, site access and excavation, structural requirements, finishes, filtration and treatment, heating, covers, plant space, landscaping and specialist features. Indoor pools add building, ventilation, dehumidification and thermal-performance considerations.

Build the budget from components, not a headline number

Pool structureConcrete, shell or lined system; size, depth and geometry.
GroundworksAccess, excavation, spoil removal, drainage and structural conditions.
Finish & featuresInterior finish, coping, lighting, steps, covers, jets and automation.
Plant & energyPumps, filters, treatment, heating, controls and plant-room access.
Surrounding worksLandscaping, paving, retaining work and reinstatement.
Indoor environmentPool hall, ventilation, dehumidification and thermal performance where relevant.

Questions every quote should answer

  • What is explicitly included and excluded?
  • Who handles design, structural engineering and service coordination?
  • What assumptions have been made about access, ground conditions and utilities?
  • What equipment, controls, cover and heating system are included?
  • What commissioning, training, warranty and aftercare are included?

Why we are not publishing one “average UK price” yet

KRONATRIX will only publish a headline price benchmark when the underlying dataset and method are strong enough to defend. Until then, a transparent cost-driver framework is more useful than copying inconsistent figures from unrelated projects.
