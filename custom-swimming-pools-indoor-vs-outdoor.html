INDOOR VS OUTDOOR

Indoor vs outdoor swimming pools: which fits your project?

Indoor pools can support more predictable year-round use but introduce a pool-hall building and environmental-control system. Outdoor pools avoid the enclosed pool-hall environment but are more exposed to weather and seasonal conditions. The right choice depends on how you will use the pool, site, budget and maintenance expectations.

Explore pool researchFor pool businesses
Direct answer: Choose indoor versus outdoor by comparing intended use, property layout, planning/building constraints, energy and environmental systems, weather exposure, privacy, maintenance and total project complexity—not just the pool itself.
