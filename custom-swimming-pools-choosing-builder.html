CHOOSING A BUILDER

How do you choose a swimming pool builder?

A strong pool-builder decision should be based on relevant evidence, a clear technical and commercial scope, who is responsible for design and specialist coordination, how changes are controlled, and what happens at commissioning and aftercare.

Explore pool researchFor pool businesses
Direct answer: Choose a pool builder by checking relevant completed work, the proposed construction system, design and engineering responsibilities, detailed inclusions and exclusions, programme assumptions, insurance and contract terms, payment stages, warranties, commissioning and aftercare. Membership of a recognised trade body can be another useful signal, but should not replace project-specific due diligence.

Builder comparison checklist

Relevant evidenceProjects similar in type, complexity and setting.
Scope clarityWritten inclusions, exclusions, assumptions and change process.
Technical ownershipWho designs structure, hydraulics, electrics, heating and indoor environment systems?
Project managementWho coordinates other trades and dependencies?
Commercial termsContract, payment stages, insurance and warranty position.
AftercareCommissioning, training, servicing and support.

Useful questions to ask before signing

  • Can I see completed projects comparable to mine?
  • What is not included in this quotation?
  • Who is responsible for planning/building-control checks?
  • Who signs off the technical design and electrical work?
  • What happens if ground conditions or access assumptions change?
  • What is the handover, commissioning and service process?
